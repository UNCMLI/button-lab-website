# Illustration Prompt 06: Therapeutic Approaches

## Intended website section

Research Area 6: Therapeutic Approaches

## Scientific story to communicate

Show a rational multimodal therapeutic strategy for defective mucus clearance: alter mucus material properties, restore airway hydration, and add mechanical energy when needed. The image should communicate combination therapy and functional clearance rather than a catalog of drugs.

## Reference concepts from Button Lab work and current programs

- Hypertonic saline improves mucociliary clearance in chronic bronchitis.
- CFTR restoration and ion-transport modulation can normalize mucus transport in selected contexts.
- Mucus-targeting approaches include hydration, reducing agents, mucinases, DNase, surfactants, and mechanical adjuncts.
- PPG Project 3: dissolution must be coupled to sustained hydration to avoid re-concentration; mechanical interventions can overcome static diffusion limits.

## Primary generation prompt

Create a polished translational biomedical illustration, horizontal 16:9 photorealistic, centered on one partially obstructed conducting airway shown in an elegant cutaway. Depict three coordinated therapeutic streams converging on a dense mucus mass: first, a blue aqueous hydration stream replenishes airway-surface liquid and separates mucus from the epithelial surface; second, a warm gold molecular-treatment stream gently opens and loosens the mucin network at the mucus surface; third, a subtle mechanical-energy field, represented by evenly spaced soft wave arcs, promotes solvent penetration and mobilization without violence. These streams should converge into one clinical outcome: mucus becomes a thinner, mobile teal sheet that can be propelled by cilia and swept by gentle airflow. Include a small nonliteral nebulizer silhouette or aerosol plume at the margin, but keep the airway cross section central. The composition must make clear that the therapies work together, not as isolated arrows. Use deep UNC navy, Carolina blue and aqua for hydration, pale gold for active treatment, restrained coral only for the untreated dense plug, and soft green for restored clearance. Warm off-white background, upper-right negative space for copy, no labels or product names.

## Optional alternate prompt

Create a clean three-pronged therapeutic schematic around a mucus plug: hydration restores the periciliary liquid layer, mucin-targeted therapy reduces network cohesion, and mechanical energy accelerates access and mobilization. The three colored streams merge at a right-side endpoint showing cilia and cough clearance of mobile mucus.

## Negative prompt

Do not draw pills, branded inhalers, drug names, syringes, a single miracle cure, a generic pharmaceutical advertisement, or a dramatic exploding mucus plug. Do not depict Compound 1155 or any proprietary compound identifier. Avoid DNA editing imagery unless used only as a tiny abstract background motif.

## Art-direction checklist

- Combination logic must be visually clear.
- Hydration should visibly deepen the periciliary/ASL space.
- Material modification should loosen the mucus mesh, not erase it.
- Mechanical energy should be gentle, spatially focused, and clinically plausible.
