# Illustration Prompt 05: Mucus Plug Pathogenesis & Clearance

## Intended website section

Research Area 5: Mucus Plug Pathogenesis & Clearance

## Scientific story to communicate

Show the full obstruction-paradigm arc: a hyperconcentrated mucus plug occludes a bronchiole, its osmotic load compresses the periciliary layer and distends the airway wall, then a combined dissolution-plus-hydration intervention opens the plug and permits clearance and wall recovery. This should be the most conceptually ambitious illustration in the set.

## Reference concepts from Button Lab work and PPG Project 3

- Gel-on-brush model: mucus hyperconcentration collapses the periciliary layer.
- Project 3 Aim 1: plug osmotic pressure causes sustained airway-wall distension and remodeling.
- Project 3 Aim 2: dissolution is diffusion-limited; surface softening is not the same as complete dissolution.
- Project 3 Aim 3: chemical plus mechanical dissolution strategies must be paired with sustained hydration to prevent re-concentration and enable clearance.
- Models: betaENaC mouse, HDM-betaENaC mouse, and cough-competent sheep validation.

## Primary generation prompt

Create a sophisticated 3D photorealistic 16:9 scientific illustration showing a causal left-to-right arc inside a medium-to-small conducting airway. Left: an initially open bronchiole has a thin hydrated mucus layer and a protected ciliated epithelium. Center-left: show a dense, dark teal hyperconcentrated mucus plug occupying most of the lumen, with tightly entangled mucin mesh texture. The plug should visibly compress the pale-blue periciliary layer and apply a sustained osmotic mechanical load across the epithelium, represented by subtle radial pressure contours or inward-outward force geometry without numbers. The airway wall around the plug should appear gently distended, with a larger lumen diameter and stressed epithelial contour, not inflamed red tissue. Center-right: show a focused combination intervention acting on the plug: a soft gold chemical-fluid front enters from the exposed surface while gentle cyclic mechanical waves or a focused ultrasound field loosen the outer plug network. Make a clear dissolution-front gradient: softened peripheral material, persistent dense core, then opening. Right: sustained blue hydration maintains the opened lumen while cilia and a modest airflow/cough stream clear fragmented mobile mucus; the airway wall returns toward a normal shape with restrained green recovery cues. Use elegant 3D vector rendering, navy airway contours, blue hydration, teal mucus, coral only for pathological stress, gold for active therapy, and green for recovery. Large clean negative space at upper right. No text, labels, equations, logos, organs outside the airway, or exaggerated explosive plug breakup.

## Negative prompt

Do not depict the plug as a blood clot, tumor, foreign body, infection-only lesion, or green slime. Do not make dissolution look instantaneous or complete after a single treatment. Do not suggest hydration alone is sufficient in a hyperabsorptive airway. Do not include Compound 1155 by name, structure, or any business-sensitive detail.

## Art-direction checklist

- The plug must be an airway-lumen mass, not a wall lesion.
- Distinguish surface softening/dissolution from an unchanged dense core.
- Show sustained hydration after opening, not merely initial solvent delivery.
- Recovery should be plausible and early-stage, not a magically pristine airway.
